# Credits and licence

Junction Studio interoperates directly with Town Road Lane by mxerf:
https://github.com/mxerf/town-road-lane

Town Road Lane is GPL-3.0. Junction Studio does not bundle its DLL, but the
source directly uses Town Road Lane's public marking data model. Junction
Studio is therefore distributed under GPL-3.0 in this source suite rather than
making a broad permissive-licence compatibility claim.
